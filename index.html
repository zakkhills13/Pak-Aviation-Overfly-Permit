Documentation Guide

Documents Required for Pakistan Permits

Ensure your application is complete to avoid delays. Our team will review all documents for accuracy before submission.

Overflight Permit Documents

  • Operator's Air Operator Certificate (AOC)
  • Aircraft Certificate of Registration
  • Certificate of Airworthiness (C of A)
  • Third-Party Liability Insurance Certificate
  • Flight Schedule / Routing Details
  • Crew List with nationalities & passport numbers
  • Radio Station License

Landing Permit Documents

  • All Overflight documents (above)
  • Noise Certificate (if applicable)
  • General Declaration (GenDec)
  • Passenger/Cargo manifest
  • Details of handling agent in Pakistan
  • Purpose of flight declaration
  • Dangerous Goods declaration (if applicable)

Diplomatic / Special Mission

  • Diplomatic note / official request letter
  • State aircraft authorization from home authority
  • Passenger list with diplomatic status
  • Security clearance documentation
  • Aircraft registration & operator details
  • Mission brief / purpose of visit
  • Contact details for protocol officer

Document Submission Timeline

For standard overflight and landing permits, documents should be submitted at minimum 72 hours prior to the planned flight. For urgent/last-minute requests, contact our operations team immediately via WhatsApp — we offer expedited processing subject to authority availability. Diplomatic clearances may require 5–7 working days advance notice.
